Saturn V Presentation

Date: Aug 2026 - Sep 2026

Summary:

  • I was inspired by miraculous engineering feats that went into the Saturn V rocket and so I created an interactive presentation that attempts to do justice to the scale, complexity and audacity of this machine.
  • Built the Apollo 11 command and service module and launch escape tower in code, working from the Apollo Operations Handbook and mission photos.
  • Animated real mission sequences: the spacecraft docking with the lunar module and pulling it free, and every piece falling away in Apollo 11's order until only the capsule that came home is left.
  • Put the numbers to scale: side-by-side comparisons with the Soviet N1 and the Statue of Liberty, NASA vs. SpaceX timelines, and an interactive map of where each stage was built and how it reached the launch pad.
